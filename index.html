Pullen Park

Pullen Park is a park in Raleigh, North Carolina, near NC State's campus. It's one of the oldest public parks in the South and a popular spot for families and students.

If you're visiting Raleigh, Pullen Park is a nice way to spend an afternoon. It doesn't cost much to get in and there's a lot to do there.

Things to Do

  • Ride the historic carousel
  • Rent a paddle boat on the lake
  • Walk or bike the trails
  • Visit the Pullen Park Aquatic Center
  • Take a ride on the mini train

Plan Your Visit

Most rides cost a little money but parking is free. The park is open all year, but ride hours change depending on the season, so it helps to check before you go.

For hours, prices, and more info, visit the official Pullen Park website.
